About Brooklyn Pro Contractors

Proudly serving Brooklyn neighborhoods for over 18 years. We're not just contractors — we're your neighbors.

Our Story

Built in Brooklyn.
Building Brooklyn.

Brooklyn Pro Contractors was founded in 2005 by Miguel Santos, a second-generation mason from Sunset Park who learned the trade from his father. Starting with a single crew and a used van, Miguel built the company on a simple principle: do the work right the first time, treat every client like a neighbor, and never walk away from a job you'd be ashamed of.

Over 18 years, that approach grew a small crew into a 32-person operation serving every corner of Brooklyn — from Greenpoint row houses to Bay Ridge co-ops to Flatbush brownstones. We've completed over 2,400 projects without losing sight of why we started.

We're fully embedded in the community we serve. Our crew members live in Brooklyn, our kids go to school here, and we take pride in making this borough stronger, safer, and more beautiful one project at a time.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the principles behind every decision we make.

🎯

Quality Without Compromise

We use premium materials, hire skilled tradespeople, and refuse to cut corners — even when it costs more time or money. Our 20-year warranty backs every word of that.

🤝

Honest & Transparent

No bait-and-switch estimates, no hidden fees, no surprise change orders. We tell you exactly what we'll do, how long it'll take, and what it'll cost — before we start.

🏘️

Community-First

We hire locally, pay fair wages, and give back to Brooklyn. When the neighborhood prospers, so do we. We've been involved in community repair initiatives across the borough since 2008.

⚡

Responsive & Reliable

We answer calls, show up on time, and finish on schedule. If something unexpected comes up, we tell you immediately — not after it's already a problem.
